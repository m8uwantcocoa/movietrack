Who Are We?
We are a team of movie enthusiasts dedicated to bringing you the best movie and TV show recommendations.
Why Did We Create This?
We created this platform to help people discover new movies and TV shows based on their preferences and ratings and because we needed to create this website as part of our Webbstjänster course to put our learning into practice and showcase our abilities as a team. With four second-year System Developer students and one first-year Information Architect student, we saw an opportunity to combine our diverse skills and create something meaningful. Our goal was not only to fulfill the course requirements but also to challenge ourselves, apply the concepts we’ve learned, and build a website that serves as a testament to our growth and creativity. This project is an important step in our journey as future professionals in the tech industry, and we are proud to present it as a collaborative effort.
Are We Non-Profit?
MovieTrack is a unique platform designed for movie lovers who want to discover more than just films. It allows users to search for movies, view detailed information including ratings, and explore the matching soundtracks that accompany each film. Whether you're looking for a specific song from a movie or just curious about its soundtrack, MovieTrack provides an easy way to find tracks you loved while watching. Additionally, the site displays the movie’s ratings and integrates with Spotify to show relevant albums, allowing you to listen to the soundtrack directly. With MovieTrack, you can explore both movies and their music in one seamless experience..
